GLAS3112 · SPAIN 2027

Read the places.
Meet the questions.

How does a place tell its story? Who translates it, preserves it — and gives it new meaning?

This field trip connects Spain’s monumental heritage with its everyday life. Explore museums, neighbourhoods and historic sites, collect evidence, and develop your own interpretation for a defined audience.

*Academic structure, assessment and activities are proposed and subject to approval. Programme, dates and estimates remain subject to confirmation.

BEFORE YOU GO

A shared journey.
Clear responsibilities.

Good preparation leaves more room
for discovery.

Travel & accommodation

Plan for shared rooms of 2–4 people and travel by public transport. The budget allows for 14 nights; the final allocation and reservations are pending.

Check passport validity, entry requirements and your own visa arrangements with the relevant official authorities before booking.

Learning & conduct

Attend preparation and safety briefings, join compulsory activities, respect local communities and follow the approved course code of conduct.

Ask before photographing or recording people. Keep notes and evidence organised for your work.

Everyday essentials

Bring comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, a refillable water bottle, suitable layers, your medication and a phone with a reliable data plan.

Keep travel documents and emergency information accessible offline. Detailed check-in and emergency procedures will be provided before departure.

Is Spanish required?

Prior Spanish is not essential under the proposed arrangements. English can be used for academic and assessed work, with support for activities involving Spanish.

Are the itinerary and visits confirmed?

No. The route and learning activities are indicative. Daily timings, reservations, accommodation and institutional exchanges remain subject to confirmation.

How can I apply?

The application procedure and deadlines have not yet been announced here. Contact the coordinators and check this site for confirmed next steps. Expressing interest does not confirm a place.
